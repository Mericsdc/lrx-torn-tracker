The local adapter is supplied as source in ../src/main/userscripts.js,
../userscript-extension/ and ../torn-helpers/session-guard.js.
The generated extension wrappers of FF Scouter and OpenMarket are distributed under GPL-3.0.
SHA-256 hashes and pinned upstream source versions are in sources.json.
These scripts have their own display settings. FF Scouter automatically uses
the tracker's saved FFScouter key, falling back to its Torn API key. Requests
use an authenticated local proxy; the saved key is not copied into the page.
TornW3B synchronizes viewed auction listings to weav3r.dev, as described by
the upstream script. OpenMarket sends nothing anywhere: it reads the Item Market
listings the page already loads and labels the visible items.
Each integration can be disabled in Settings / Torn helpers.

Credits for ideas (no code copied; the tracker's helpers are written from scratch):
the bazaar undercut helper (../torn-helpers/bazaar-undercut.js) follows the idea of
"Auto Bazaar Pricing" by swervelord [3637232]. Its prices come from weav3r.dev.
The profile helper (../torn-helpers/profile-work-stats.js) follows the idea of
"Torn Profile Company Star Rating + Working Stats" by swervelord. Its numbers come
from the Torn API, looked up by the tracker with its own saved key.
