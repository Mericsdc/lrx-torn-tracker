# LRX Torn Tracker v1.6.19

## Travel Workspace, Faction Travel Traffic & 24h Departure Clock Dial (N1 & N2)

- **Unified Travel Workspace (Free):** Switch seamlessly between **By Item**, **By Country**, and **Travel ROI** views with customizable block visibility (`Summary`, `Country Cards`, `Faction Traffic`, `History Chart`, `24h Planner`, `Destinations`) and availability filters (`Reachable on arrival`, `In stock now`, `Restock soon`, `All`).
- **Country Cards & Item Summary:** 11 interactive country cards distinguish verified `0` stock (`Sold out`) from unobserved data (`Unknown`) and mark observations older than 30 minutes as `Stale data`. The Item Summary card breaks down reference net profit across `Item Market (−5%)`, `Bazaar (0%)`, and `NPC Shop (0%)` with 24h price/stock ranges.
- **6/12/24/48h Stock, Price, Profit & Forecast Chart:** Step-line SVG chart with `▲` verified restock markers, `YATA ↔ Prometheus` source-switch indicators, and dashed depletion/restock forecast series.
- **Faction Travel Traffic & 24h Clock Dial:** Tracks `Outbound`, `Abroad`, and `Inbound` faction traffic (deduplicated per member per snapshot, with `Unknown route` counted separately) and renders an interactive 24-hour TCT SVG Clock Dial synchronized with the chronological flight list, **Pin Plan** validation, and **Flight & Purchase History**.

## Faction Members Drawer, Personal Charts & Extended Workspace (N3, N4 & N5)

- **Faction › Members & Personal Progression Charts (Free):** Full roster table loaded from the shared roster cache with zero extra per-member Torn API calls, column picker, and keyboard-accessible inline member detail drawer (`Battle Stats`, `Activity`, `Gym Contribution`, `Travel Transitions`, `Value | Gain` mode, `7d` to `All` ranges). Single total estimates (e.g. FFScouter) are never synthetically split into sub-stats.
- **Detailed Faction Tabs (Free):** Added **Stats** (7d/30d/90d deltas with membership tenure filter and explicit active-rate denominator), **Faction Gym** (verified contributors & share `%`), **Bank** (read-only balances + Torn Faction Bank link), **Applications & Recruitment Watchlist**, **Transfers** (explicit `[seenFrom → seenTo]` observation intervals), and **Alliance, Family & Graffiti**.
- **Overview Widgets (N5):** Added 9 Faction and Travel Overview widgets (`faction_member`, `faction_activity`, `faction_stats`, `faction_radar`, `travel_routes`, `travel_stock`, `travel_traffic`, `travel_planner`, `travel_history`) with shared endpoint caching and direct deep-linking.

## Update

Existing users can install through the app's update button. Local keys, settings and profiles are preserved. New users can download `LRX-Torn-Tracker-Windows-x64.zip`, extract it and run TornTracker.exe.
