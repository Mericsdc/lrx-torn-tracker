# LRX Torn Tracker v1.6.13

## Safer updates and add-ons

- An update that fails half-way now puts the previous version back by itself. A broken or incomplete download never changes your install.
- TornTools is checked the way Chrome checks it: the package must carry TornTools' own signature, otherwise it is not installed and the version you already have keeps running.
- A Torn tab that keeps changing pages on its own (more than 8 times a minute while you are not touching it) is stopped until you click in the page. New setting **Settings → App → Stop self-navigating tabs**; very fast loops are always stopped.

## Fixes

- **Xanax Today** is exact only when the app saw your count shortly before the 18:00 TCT day change. Otherwise the chip says so: `≥` when counting started after you opened the app, `≤` when some may have been taken the day before.
- Saving a new API key in Settings no longer reports a timeout while the key waits for the Torn API rate limit.
- Prices such as "1,500k" or "2.500m" are refused instead of being read as a much smaller number. This protects the purchase price guard.
- FFScouter: a key that is not registered is reported as not registered, not as an outage.
- Poker odds show your win chance, the split pot chance and your expected share of the pot, instead of a misleading "tie %".
- Clearer messages when the update server, the feedback service or the Torn API cannot be reached, and when a refresh fails.
- Hospital alerts also fire when you or a target leave hospital early (a revive or a medical item), so an early release is not missed. Battle stats mark which total applies right now, with or without Xanax.
- The Premium page has a new layout, and its messages now stay in view at the bottom of the window.
- Torn tabs stay lined up with the top bars when the app is zoomed, Reload acts on the tab you are viewing, open tabs survive a reload of the app page, and the app page comes back by itself if it ever crashes.

## Update

Existing users can install through the app's update button. Local keys, settings and profiles are preserved. New users can download `LRX-Torn-Tracker-Windows-x64.zip`, extract it and run TornTracker.exe.
