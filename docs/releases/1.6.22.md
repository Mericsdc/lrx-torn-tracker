# LRX Torn Tracker v1.6.22

## Companion update

- **Company Director.** See products with less than 1.5 days of stock, the change between the last two daily revenue records, effectiveness thresholds, and the latest employee training entries found in company news. Compare weekly income with companies of the same type and star rating.
- **Bazaar & Market.** Look up an item by ID to see the first 20 official Item Market listings, their prices, quantities, and variants. Enter a purchase or bazaar price, a sale price, and a fee to estimate net proceeds and profit.
- **Trade Manager.** Read open and completed trades, look up a trade by ID, and copy a receipt. The cash preview supports dollars, percentages, and k/m/b amounts, with a minimum wallet reserve.
- **Faction Intelligence.** See your personal faction cash and points in Tools and in the Faction Balance widget for eligible Overview layouts. The Chain view shows the next achievable bonus and remaining hits.
- **Gym & Training.** See your active gym and the best available gym for Strength, Defense, Speed, and Dexterity. The comparison shows each modifier and includes ties.
- **Command Palette.** Press Ctrl+K or Alt+K in Tracker to search its views and Torn destinations. Use the arrows to choose a result, Enter to open it, Esc to close, or start with `=` to calculate.
- **Inventory.** Pick from 25 Torn item categories and browse pages of up to 20 entries. See quantities, equipped and faction-owned markers, and a shortcut to the Torn inventory.
- **Live Status & Timers.** Energy and nerve show their next recovery and full-bar countdowns while below maximum. The bar value updates when the API confirms it. Overview also shows drug, education, daily reset, booster and medical cooldowns, and landing time during travel.
- **Chat.** Copy a multiline draft from Tools. The optional Torn chat highlighter marks keyword matches in the chat you are viewing. It clears the highlights when the window loses focus, the tab changes, or the feature is turned off. Highlighting is off by default.
- **Weapons & Vault.** See equipped gear, rarity and bonuses where available, plus your personal vault and wallet balances.

The new cards share the official Torn API connection and local cache. Cash and market calculators are previews. Trade confirmation, item use, equipment changes, transfers, and chat sending remain manual actions in Torn.

Use the in-app update button. Your local key, data, and settings are preserved. For a fresh Windows installation, download `LRX-Torn-Tracker-Windows-x64.zip`, extract it, and run `TornTracker.exe`.

Feature inspiration came from TornW3B Companion, The Swiss Army Knife, Torn Trade - Add & Withdraw, and TornCast.
