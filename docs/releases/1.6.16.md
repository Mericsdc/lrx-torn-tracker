# LRX Torn Tracker v1.6.16

## Ranked War Attack fix & OC Armory shortcut

- **Ranked War Attack button (`page.php`):** Updated the attack link injected on Torn's Ranked War page (as well as Missions and Racing links in the Merit Guide) from the retired `loader.php` endpoint to `page.php`, fixing the `"This endpoint is no longer available"` error.
- **OC Missing Item → Armory Utilities:** Clicking a row with a missing item in Ongoing OCs, clicking the missing-item badge on Your OC, or clicking a missing-item desktop notification now opens Torn's Faction Armory Utilities page directly.

## Travel Departure Planner

- Added a collapsible **Departure Planner** above the Travel Profit table with four live HUD cards (**Next Flight** with live countdown, required cash, shortage and high-carry warning, **Profit** today / after midnight, **Per Hour** while flying, and **Biggest Carry**).
- Added an interactive **24-hour timeline** with **Profit / Cash to take** views, in-flight return tracking, **Market −5% / Bazaar 0% / NPC 0%** pricing modes, **Max $ / Max $/hr / Short flights** optimization, and click-to-remove stops.

## Stock Market, War Payouts, Company Optimizer & Faction Intel

- **Stock Market page:** Live stock prices, annual ROI % for cash, live-priced item and points benefit blocks, doubling-increment marginal ROI %, holdings P/L net of Torn's 0.1% sell fee, Bank APR comparison, and locally recorded 30-day observed price range.
- **Ranked War Payout Planner:** Pool or fixed-rate payout calculation with respect/hits weighting, faction cut %, minimum hits, per-member caps with proportional overflow redistribution, prefilled Torn Faction Bank links, and Excel-safe CSV export.
- **Company Position Optimizer:** Optimal employee-to-role matching on the Employees page using Torn's working-stat efficiency formula, editable target role counts, and a `BUS2110 (+20%)` toggle.
- **Faction Intel tabs:** Added **Activity** (live Online/Idle/Offline distribution, passively collected 7×24 TCT heatmap, full filterable roster, 2-member comparison, and 7D/30D report switch), **Armory** history, **Chains** reports, and an interactive **Upgrades** respect simulator (`Core 1×`, active branches `1×→32×`).

## Update

Existing users can install through the app's update button. Local keys, settings and profiles are preserved. New users can download `LRX-Torn-Tracker-Windows-x64.zip`, extract it and run TornTracker.exe.
