# LRX Torn Tracker v1.6.14

## Your Overview, your way

- The Overview is now a grid of cards around your profile card. Press **Edit** to drag cards, add or remove them, use the keyboard, or reset. Your layout is saved for each Torn player you sign in with. To get the old screen back, turn off **Overview layout** in Settings.
- A new **Live timers** strip shows your drug cooldown, education and the 18:00 TCT day reset in one line, with a LIVE / DELAYED badge so you can see how fresh the data is.
- The Merit card on the Overview now points at your easiest next merits.

## New: Beginner Guide and Happy jump

- Players below level 15 get a **Beginner Guide**: a step-by-step roadmap for gym, happy jump, crimes, job, education and more, with tips based on your own numbers.
- From level 15 the **Happy jump** card in Tools gives a live readiness checklist and a step-by-step guide. It only shows information; you take every step yourself.

## New: Finance page

- **Networth** and **Income Statement** are now one **Finance** page with two tabs. **Networth** shows how your wealth is split, how it changed over a period and its history. **Company** shows the income statement for directors.

## Cleaner pages

- **Stock Status**: four summary tiles, one delivery line with an order-time menu, and a product table (cards in narrow windows).
- **Employees**: a calmer page. The rehab message lists only employees at -8 or worse and writes one polite message for you to copy.
- **Merit Tracker**: an **Easy wins** card, a **Show** filter by group, and a line under every award that says what to do and how much is left.
- **Gym & Stats** fits on one screen and the chart is a sensible size. **Daily Report** no longer repeats the order list.
- **Premium** and **Themes** are redesigned. Themes now has a list with a large live preview.
- The left sidebar slides its highlight to the page you open. Like every animation it is off with reduced motion or with **Animations** turned off.

## Torn helpers (display only, each one can be turned off in Settings)

- **OpenMarket 1.11.9** by snickelfritz / cowboyup: quality and bonus labels on the Item Market, bazaars and the Auction House. Its sort-menu entries and sliders are switched off so nothing presses Torn's controls.
- **Bazaar −$1**: on Bazaar → Manage and Add items, a button next to each item fills that item's price box with the cheapest listing from weav3r.dev minus $1. Your own listings are ignored, and you still press Update yourself. Idea credit: "Auto Bazaar Pricing" by swervelord.
- **Working stats on profiles**: a player's working stats and their company's star rating appear above Life. This makes two public Torn API requests per profile with your saved key, kept in memory only. Idea credit: swervelord.

## New Year

- During Christmas Town the sign-in logo wears a Santa hat with ice and lights, and light snow falls behind the cards.
- The first time you open the app on 1 January, confetti and fireworks celebrate the new year. There is no midnight countdown.

## Fixes

- Signing in with Google through Chrome now replaces every old Torn cookie in the app. Before, a leftover session cookie could make Torn answer "Wrong rfcv token" or "Validation is required" on most pages until the app was restarted.

## Update

Existing users can install through the app's update button. Local keys, settings and profiles are preserved. New users can download `LRX-Torn-Tracker-Windows-x64.zip`, extract it and run TornTracker.exe.
