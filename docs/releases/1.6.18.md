# LRX Torn Tracker v1.6.18

## Faction Travel Radar & Shared Roster Cache

- **Faction › Travel Radar (Free):** Tracks faction members flying outbound, staying abroad, or returning to Torn with clickable summary filters, search, country/activity filters, Torn profile links, and one-click Country → Travel Profit cross-links.
- **Strict ETA & Separate Axes:** Activity (`Online / Idle / Offline`) and health (`Okay / Hospital / Jail`) are tracked independently. Flight ETA windows appear only from observed transitions or verified flight arrival fields; hospital/jail timers are never shown as flight ETAs.
- **Shared Roster Cache:** Activity, Travel Radar, OC, and Member Reports share a single 2-minute Torn roster cache with in-flight deduplication, explicit freshness timestamps (TCT + age, stale after 5 min), strict account/faction isolation, and immediate private-cache wipe on auth revocation.

## Charts, Alerts & Cross-Module Links

- **24h TCT Activity Step-Line:** Faction Activity now plots a 24-hour TCT step-line chart with explicit member denominator and visible outage gap bands instead of interpolated zeroes.
- **Stock Market Price Threshold Alerts:** Set local above/below price thresholds per stock; the first observation sets the baseline, source outages never emit false alerts, and clicking an alert jumps directly to that stock row.
- **War Score Comparison & Cross-Links:** Ranked War Payout Planner shows a 2-series score comparison bar with exact score difference and War ID; Chains link to War Payout, and away OC members jump to Travel Radar while preserving OC Missing Item → Armory Utilities.

## Control & Operations Screen + Shared Faction Hub

- **Settings › General › Control & Operations:** Displays live Torn, YATA, and Prometheus source health (latency, call counts, error class, masked key), local roster freshness, optional Faction Hub collector coverage/pause/resume/refresh controls, notification outbox status, and audit logs.
- **Shared Faction Hub Service & Team Plan Backend:** Separate SQLite database with encrypted collector keys, Torn-verified officer/member roles, lease-fenced snapshot ingest, independent 5-minute collector worker, permission-capped Discord outbox (`needs_review` on uncertain timeouts), and faction-scoped Team Plans.

## Update

Existing users can install through the app's update button. Local keys, settings and profiles are preserved. New users can download `LRX-Torn-Tracker-Windows-x64.zip`, extract it and run TornTracker.exe.
