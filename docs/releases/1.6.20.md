# LRX Torn Tracker v1.6.20

A fix release for the Travel, Faction, Stock Market and Overview features added in 1.6.18 and 1.6.19.

## Stock Market

- A price alert that fires no longer breaks the Stock Market page, and its notification is not lost. Alerts are checked while the Stock Market page is open; the setting now says so.

## Travel Profit

- Times marked **TCT** are Torn time again (they showed your computer's local time). Times that are not today also show the date.
- **Add to plan** puts the route into the 24h departure planner as the next flight. It no longer changes your country filter. Remove it from the banner, the row ✕ or the button.
- **By Country** is its own view: items are grouped per country, in flight-time order.
- The history chart keeps 48 hours in 30-minute steps, so 6h to 48h and the 24h price/stock range cover what they say.
- Country cards show the restock time of the item they show. A country reads **Sold out** only when nothing profitable is in stock, and a country with no data reads **Unknown** only.
- **Flight & Purchase History** lists only purchases made abroad, and says whether a log key is set and checked.
- A pinned plan ends after its last return instead of showing yesterday's flights.
- Faction traffic on the Travel page follows the Faction page and Travel Radar switches. The new **Travel workspace** switch brings back the classic layout.
- Flight time estimates for faction members cover every ticket type instead of assuming Standard. Members in a hospital or jail abroad count as abroad.
- English mode no longer shows Turkish number and percent formats.

## Faction

- The member travel log records every flight with its country (it kept only the first one).
- The Members table and the Overview Travel Radar widget show the destination and arrival time again.
- Travel Radar refreshes every 2 minutes while the Faction page is open.
- Text you type in the Recruitment watchlist, Alliance or Graffiti forms is no longer wiped by a refresh. Alliance and Graffiti notes are labelled as stored only on this computer.
- 30d and 90d columns and the Stats tab show how many days of data they really cover. History is kept for 95 days so 90d can fill.
- Bank and Applications say when Torn could not be reached, instead of "Permission needed" or "No pending applications". Players without a faction get a clear message.
- Battle stats say whether a value is verified, an FFScouter estimate or a rough estimate from rank. Other members' Battle Stats charts build their history from FFScouter totals the app already loaded.
- The Shared source no longer shows "undefined" in Activity.

## Settings and Overview

- **Control & Operations** has its own switch and loads only when Settings › General is open. Torn permission errors no longer mark Torn as down, and the team plan date no longer reads 1970.
- Two notification switches that did nothing (war events, company 18:00 summary) were removed.
- Overview widgets no longer show the previous account's data after you switch accounts.
- With Premium switched off, the Faction Hub screens send nothing to the Premium service.

## Shared Faction Hub (server, not enabled yet)

- Connecting a faction checks the Leader's or Co-leader's own key. Reading the hub never calls Torn. Members who leave are removed, old data is cleaned up daily, and the privacy text describes what the hub stores. The hub stays off on the live server until it is set up.

## Update

Existing users can install through the app's update button. Local keys, settings and profiles are preserved. New users can download `LRX-Torn-Tracker-Windows-x64.zip`, extract it and run TornTracker.exe.
