🔑 Torn Login & Session Import (for Google Accounts)

Why does Google say "Couldn't sign you in / This browser or app may not be secure"?
Google blocks Google-account sign-in inside browsers embedded in desktop .exe apps (Electron). So TornTracker has you sign in with Google in Chrome/Edge on your computer and then imports the session into the app automatically.

Method 1 · Sign in with Google (via Chrome/Edge) — Recommended

Press the button → Chrome (or Edge if Chrome isn't installed) opens Torn in a separate window just for TornTracker → sign in with Google there → once your Torn home page appears, close that window. When it closes, the session is imported here. Pressing the Google (G) button on Torn also starts this flow automatically.

Method 2 · Torn Email & Password

On the Torn login screen, type your email and password instead of using the Google (G) button.

If you created your account with Google and have no Torn password, click "Forgot Password?" once on the Torn login screen to set one by email.
